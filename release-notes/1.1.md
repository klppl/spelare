Every player setting is now one click away, in the controls at the top of the video.

- **Quality, audio, subtitles and speed in the player.** The small Settings button is now labelled
  buttons that show what's playing: the quality ("1080p"), the audio language (on videos with dubs),
  the subtitles (on videos with captions) and the speed ("1.5×"). Click one to change it. On a small
  player, audio, subtitles and speed shrink to icons.
- **Theater mode works.** The Theater button often didn't show at all; now it's always there on
  youtube.com, with arrows that show whether it makes the video wider or smaller.
- Larger, easier-to-read menus.
