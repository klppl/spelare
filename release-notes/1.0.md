The first release of Spelare: YouTube in Safari's own video player.

- **Safari's player.** Videos play in Safari's native player with YouTube's HLS streams, so you get
  Picture in Picture, AirPlay, media keys and low battery use, without ads.
- **Quality and sound.** Pick a quality (up to 4K) or Sound only from the player, change it with `Q`,
  choose a dubbed audio language, and turn loud videos down to an even level.
- **Chapters.** A Chapters button shows the current chapter and jumps to any other.
- **Picks up where you left off.** Videos resume where you stopped, volume is remembered, and speed is
  remembered per channel.
- **Captions** with auto-translation, on by default if you like.
- **A cleaner YouTube.** Optionally hide Shorts, recommendations, comments and end screens, and show
  dislike counts from Return YouTube Dislike.
- **iCloud sync** of settings, per-channel speeds and resume positions between your devices.
